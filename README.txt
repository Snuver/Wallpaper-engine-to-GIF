# Wallpaper GIF Converter v1.4
# Made by Snuver
============================

I made this because somehow converting wallpaper engine clips into a normal GIF
is way more annoying than it should be lol

What it does
------------
Throw a wallpaper engine ZIP/folder/project.json at it and it will try to turn
that wallpaper into a GIF!!!!!


Video wallpaper?
Easy. It converts the video.

Scene wallpaper?
It makes Wallpaper Engine render it, records the actual visible wallpaper area,
then turns that recording into a GIF.

Why does it record the screen area?
-----------------------------------
Because wallpaper engine is GPU rendered and normal window capture can literally
record a black box even while the wallpaper looks perfectly fine on your screen.
So yeah. Desktop region capture it is.

How to use it?
-------------
1. Extract this folder.
2. Drag your Wallpaper Engine ZIP onto WE-To-GIF-v1.4.bat.
3. Press ENTER on the settings if you do not care and want my recommended ones. (Not accurate, its better if you do it your own)
4. Do NOT cover or minimize the Wallpaper Engine pop-out while it records.
5. Done.

What I recommend
----------------
10 seconds
12 FPS
1280 px wide

You can use 30 FPS if you want. Just know GIF file sizes become stupidly huge
really fast.

Where is my GIF?
----------------
It saves here:

C:\Users\YOUR_NAME\Videos\Wallpaper GIF Converter\

Supported right now
-------------------
- Wallpaper Engine Scene wallpapers
- Video wallpapers
- Normal video files

Not supported yet
-----------------
- Web wallpapers
